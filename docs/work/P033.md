### B1. Register

- **Tables:** `mg_model` and `mg_model_use`, with history, holding every field of design section 3.
- **Materiality:** one of high, medium or low, with a reason; the app gives the default. Status is one of proposed, validated, live or retired, with the last validation and the next review due.
- **The model:** provider is `VENDOR` or `CUSTOMER`. Its digest is an algorithm plus a value; give a SHA-256 file-digest helper, plus the HTTP model server's manifest digest as an optional adapter (a common local model server API, not any product).
- **A use's configuration fingerprint:** SHA-256 of the app's canonical JSON of its prompt and settings. The app computes the JSON; platform-core gives the canonical form and the hash.
- **Changes** are versioned, with who and when, and go to the app's audit through `ModelGovAudit`, as in integration.

### B2. Validation records and the gate

- **A record** (`mg_validation`, insert-only) holds:
  - the use, model, version, digest and configuration fingerprint;
  - the test set's name and version;
  - each measure with its result and threshold;
  - pass or fail;
  - who ran it, and when;
  - **never** content or values.
- **Thresholds:** the app's default pass marks per use, plus the customer's stricter ones per entity (`mg_threshold`). A customer threshold looser than the default is refused (`MOD_THRESHOLD_LOOSER`). The thresholds in force are copied into the record.
- **Approval:** `approve(validationId, approver)`.
  - The approver gives their identity and their roles, as the app knows them.
  - The settings name the **Model approver** role(s), and the roles that may **never** also be held. That is the Business Admin role, given by the app's configuration, not a name in platform-core code.
  - A compliance-officer role may be configured as an approver.
  - **Refusals:** the approver lacks the role (`MOD_APPROVER_ROLE`); they also hold a forbidden role (`MOD_APPROVER_CONFLICT`); the validation failed (`MOD_VALIDATION_FAILED`).
- **Customer model failing the test set** (design section 4):
  - for a **high** use, refused: `MOD_CUSTOMER_MODEL_BLOCKED`;
  - for a **medium** use, allowed only with a documented acceptance (who, when, which results, a free-text reason with no content), kept with the record.
- **Going live:** `goLive(use, entity, validationId)` makes it live; the previous live one stays as the rollback target. `rollback(use, entity)` switches back at once. Both are audited.
- **The gate at model start:** `ModelGate.requireLive(use, entity, observedDigest, observedConfigFingerprint)` returns a `LiveModel`, or refuses:
  - `MOD_NOT_VALIDATED`;
  - `MOD_NOT_APPROVED`;
  - `MOD_DIGEST_MISMATCH`: the model file or pull changed;
  - `MOD_CONFIG_CHANGED`: the prompt or settings changed without validation;
  - `MOD_RETIRED`;
  - `MOD_REVIEW_OVERDUE`: past its next review. This one is a warning by default; the customer may make it a refusal per use.

  Every refusal implements `PlatformCode` (P032), with a cause and fix for IT, and the app raises its IT notice from it.
- **Result stamping:** `LiveModel.stamp()` gives a `ModelStamp` (use, model, version, digest, validation ID, configuration fingerprint) that the app stores with each AI result. A stamp is plain data, safe to log.
