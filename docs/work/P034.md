## B. Build (in `platform-model-governance`, `-testkit`, and a new `-spring`)

### B1. Monitoring intake and thresholds

- **Reporting:** `ModelMeasures.report(use, entity, measure, value, period)`, for numbers only. `mg_measure` is insert-only, with a retention the installation sets (default 400 days).
- **Thresholds:** each measure gets one (the app's default, or the customer's stricter one), with a direction (above or below is bad) and a minimum sample size.
- **Drift:** a reading past its threshold raises a `ModelEvent.DRIFT` for the app's notifications, and the use shows **needs review** in the register until someone records what was done: an action note, who, and when.
- **Re-validation due:**
  - each use's next review date comes from its materiality (defaults high 90 days, medium 180, low 365; changeable per use);
  - `ModelReviews.due(now)` lists those coming due (30 and 7 days before, and overdue) and raises `ModelEvent.REVIEW_DUE`;
  - every change of model, version, digest, configuration or where it runs makes the use due **at once**.
- No document content or values in any measure or event.

### B2. Documentation pack export

`ModelPack.export(product, entity, from, to)` gives **JSON** (format `platform-model-pack/1`, with its schema) and **PDF**. It holds design section 6, items 1–7:
- the register entries;
- per use: purpose, design summary, inputs and outputs, human in the loop, guardrails and known limits, as the app supplies them in text;
- the validation history, with thresholds, results, approvals and acceptances;
- monitoring: measures, thresholds, drift events and actions;
- the change log, with digests and rollbacks;
- the third-party statement, including the independent review, which the app or vendor attaches as a document reference;
- the data statement.

The pack is **fingerprinted**: the SHA-256 of the JSON, printed in the PDF. The same data always gives the same JSON. For the PDF, use a library that is already a dependency somewhere in platform-core; otherwise choose a small, maintained open-source one and say why. Neither part holds document content or values.
